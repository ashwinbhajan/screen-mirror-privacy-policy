Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 22, 2026

Screen Mirror to TV Cast ("the app") helps you discover compatible TVs, connect with Chromecast and AirPlay devices, mirror your screen, and cast photos, videos, and media links.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may access, how it is used, and what choices you have.

Information We Access

  • Local Network access to discover and connect to nearby TVs and casting devices.
  • Photo Library access when you choose photos or videos to cast.
  • Microphone permission as part of the iOS screen broadcast flow. The app is not designed to record or store microphone audio.
  • Basic purchase status from Apple to unlock premium features and restore purchases.
  • Advertising-related data from ad providers when free users view ads.

How Information Is Used

  • To find compatible AirPlay, Chromecast, and smart TV devices on your network.
  • To cast the photos, videos, and media links you select.
  • To support screen mirroring through Apple's system broadcast tools.
  • To enable premium purchases, restore purchases, and manage subscription access.
  • To show ads in the free version of the app.

Data Storage

The app is designed so your selected media and local network activity stay on your device and your local network unless required by Apple, Google, or another service you explicitly use. We do not state that personal data is sold.

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services such as:

  • Apple services for purchases, AirPlay, and system broadcast features.
  • Google Cast services for Chromecast and Google TV casting.
  • Google Mobile Ads for advertising in the free version.

These providers may process data according to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

The app is not intentionally directed to children for the collection of personal information.

Your Choices

  • You can deny Local Network, Photos, or Microphone permissions in iOS Settings.
  • You can stop using premium purchases at any time through your Apple ID subscription settings.
  • You can limit ad tracking through Apple device settings where available.
