2. Device Permissions Used

The app requests the following iOS permissions solely to provide its core functionality. None of the data accessed through these permissions is collected, stored on any server I control, or shared with third parties by me.

  • Local Network: Used to discover and connect to Chromecast, Google TV, AirPlay, and other compatible devices on your Wi-Fi network. All communication stays within your local network. No network traffic from this feature reaches external servers I operate.
  • Photo Library: Accessed only when you explicitly choose a photo or video to cast. The selected media is streamed directly to your casting device on the local network. It is not uploaded to any server.
  • Microphone: iOS requires microphone permission as part of its built-in ReplayKit screen broadcast framework. The app does not independently record, process, store, or transmit microphone audio. Any audio captured during a broadcast session is handled entirely by Apple's ReplayKit system on your device.

3. Data I Do Not Collect

The following data is not collected, stored, or processed by this app:

  • Name, email address, or any account information (no account required)
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or fingerprinting data
  • Location data
  • Browsing history or usage analytics sent to external servers
  • Photos, videos, or media content
  • Crash reports or diagnostics transmitted off-device
  • Payment or financial information

4. Third-Party Services

The app integrates with the following third-party platform services to deliver its core features. Each of these processes data under their own privacy policies, which I encourage you to review:

  • Apple / AirPlay / ReplayKit: Screen mirroring and system broadcast are delivered through Apple's frameworks. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Cast SDK: Chromecast and Google TV casting is delivered through Google's Cast SDK. When you cast to a Chromecast or Google TV device, Google's SDK facilitates the connection. Google Privacy Policy

I do not integrate advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting services that transmit personal data to third parties.

5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the legal basis for the limited device-level data access described in Section 2 is:

  • Legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): Accessing your local network, photo library, and microphone permission is strictly necessary to provide the casting and mirroring functionality you have requested. This processing is proportionate and does not override your fundamental rights, since the data stays on your device and local network and is not transmitted to any server I control.

Since no personal data is collected or stored by me, most GDPR obligations (data retention, data breach notification, data processing agreements) do not apply in practice. However, I am committed to complying with applicable law.

6. Your Rights (EEA / GDPR)

As no personal data is stored by me, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. However, you retain the following rights at all times:

  • Right to withdraw permissions: You can revoke Local Network, Photo Library, and Microphone permissions at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • Right to information: You may contact me at any time to ask what data, if any, is associated with your use of the app.
  • Right to lodge a complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority. In Poland, this is the Urząd Ochrony Danych Osobowych (UODO) at uodo.gov.pl.

7. Data Retention

I do not store personal data on any server. All processing of device permissions occurs locally on your device and, where applicable, on your local network. When you uninstall the app, any locally cached preferences are permanently removed from your device.

8. Children's Privacy

9. Data Security
